# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

This project is a professional learning and review space for agent-building, automation, retrieval, and AI-assisted software development. Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, share feedback constructively, and treat differing technical perspectives in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, evidence-based, and clear about assumptions, limitations, and risks. Respect privacy, intellectual property, and the security of third-party systems. Keep reviews focused on the work, welcome constructive disagreement, and help make documentation accessible to developers at different experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and instructions intended to bypass safeguards are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately through the contact path in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md). Maintainers will review credible reports and may request a correction, remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related communication.
